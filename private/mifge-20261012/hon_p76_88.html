12 / p.87 ― MIFGE STORY（左段）+ MIFGE OFFER（右段）

動画時刻 00:53.75 区間 00:50 – 00:54.5 全文書き起こし

[左段] MIFGE STORY You'll develop a story about why the offer matters right now. Even though you have an irresistible offer, it doesn't mean you should go out and start selling it right away just because it's an amazing offer. You'll want to use a story to put a spin on your MIFGE to help it convert. Here's a few options that will help you to tell a story about your MIFGE offer. Why You Created The Offer You can share the story behind why you put this offer together. For instance, I'll be launching the Secrets of Success MIFGE which includes manuscripts no one has ever seen before! Now that's a great story! Also, when we put together the Magnetic Marketing MIFGE, I shared the story about when I bought Dan Kennedy's company and found all of his amazing trainings in his archives and how I had to get them out to everyone. Why You're Making The Offer Today You can use any holiday and create a promotion around it to make it feel urgent. It's the same offer, but you're just tweaking the promotion. An easy way to do that is to have the same landing page for your MIFGE but to change your ads and social media posts to talk about the seasonal promotion. Some holidays to create promotions around are listed below, but you can use any holiday or event to promote your MIFGE. • Your Birthday • Valentine's Day • Easter • Back to School • Halloween • Thanksgiving • Black Friday • Cyber Monday • Christmas [右段] MIFGE OFFER You'll create an offer that includes your continuity + bonuses (digital and/or physical). The first thing you should put in your MIFGE is your continuity. You can include a free 7-day, 14-day, or 30-day trial. You don't want anyone to be surprised that they are subscribing to your continuity, so the core thing you're offering is your continuity. PRO TIP: Don't do forced continuity or put your continuity on upsells or order bumps because most people don't read those carefully. You want to use words like "subscribe" front and center so they know exactly what they're signing up for, but they want your free gift so bad they don't mind trying out your continuity, too. [薄い水色のハイライト囲み] WARNING: The problem some people make is that they try to hide the continuity. DO NOT DO THIS. This is the fastest way to lose your merchant account, get upset customers, and create a ton of headaches for you. The key is to sell the continuity. The core thing you're selling is the continuity, so don't put it into the stack as a tiny bullet point that no one will see. Instead, talk about your membership benefits and why someone would want to be a member. Then show them the bonuses or bribe. I've seen too many people who try to hide the continuity in the fine print lose their businesses. I'm also not a fan of plugging continuity into an order form bump or upsell. I want it front and center so people know exactly what they are subscribing to. You'll also add in a bunch of digital and/or physical products to increase the value of the offer to push them over the fence to join your continuity. We've found that when we ship a huge box of books to our customers, not only do they love getting something in the mail, but it also helps them to stay on the continuity, so we're big fans of creating a digital + physical MIFGE (page 87)

13 / p.88 ― "Remember…" 引用＋ Digital Products ／ Physical Products

[左段 冒頭・薄い水色のハイライト囲み] Remember, more doesn't always mean better. You want everything they get to help them, not overwhelm them. Also, a MIFGE offer shouldn't be a "complete" product. As Ryan Deiss has often said, your MIFGE should be "useful, but incomplete." All of the things in my MIFGE offers are cool, they have awesome stories associated with them, and they are very useful… but the complete system, frameworks, training, etc… are all found within the actual membership program. Also, because your customers and leads are in different places on their journeys, the MIFGE is an opportunity to get everyone caught up to speed and on the same page. [左段 本文] Examples of what you might want in your MIFGE offer (in addition to joining your continuity): • Digital Products Ideally, products you've already created or could create with minimal effort · Courses · Templates · Swipe files · Virtual event tickets [右段] • Physical Products Ideally, products that are easy to ship, cost less than $10 each, and stay visible long-term · Stick Letter (The goal of this letter is to get your members to stick by bringing back their original excitement and explaining what they get in the membership.) · Postcard to upgrade to the next level (Take every chance you can to ascend your members.) · Books or workbooks (These are usually kept on tables or counters and are rarely thrown away.) · Branded clothing like t-shirts or hats (Your customers will wear their new identity with pride.) · Branded swag like backpacks or canvas tote bags (Your customers will give you free advertising.)
